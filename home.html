Our Story in Brief

Founded in 1984 by three Italian immigrants from different regions of Italy, Tre Mani Pasta House began as a small New York restaurant dedicated to preserving the traditions of handmade pasta and authentic Italian cooking. Combining family recipes, carefully sourced ingredients, and regional specialties from northern, central, and southern Italy, the founders created a dining experience centered on craftsmanship, simplicity, and genuine Italian flavors, transforming their immigrant dream into a beloved local institution.
